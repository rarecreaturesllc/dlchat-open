DLChat needs no account and no sign-in. You can test everything without a radio.

To try it: open the app, make a group (the app generates the phrase), type a message on Send and choose Words, Sound or QR. On a second device with the same phrase, Receive opens the message. Chat works over the internet between two devices in the same group.

About radios: DLChat is not a radio and does not transmit on any band by itself. It can connect over Bluetooth to a Meshtastic radio the user already owns. Meshtastic radios use license-free ISM bands (in the US, 902 to 928 MHz under FCC Part 15), where encrypted data is allowed. A user can also read the words aloud or play the tones over a voice radio. Rules for that differ by country and radio service, so the app shows a "Radio rules" notice before the first radio use and keeps it in Settings. The user is responsible for following the rules where they are.

Bluetooth is used only for the Meshtastic radio. The microphone is used only to hear message tones. The camera is used only to scan a QR code. Location is used only while the mesh map is open and stays on the phone.
