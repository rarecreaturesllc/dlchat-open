# Interaction matrix

Branch `interaction/polish`, on top of the Faceplate redesign (2026-11-11). Tokens: `DLMotion`
(`press` 200ms spring, `move` 250ms snappy, `exit` 200ms ease-out, `pressScale` 0.97) in
`DLChat/Design/Tokens.swift`; haptics: `Haptics` (`tap`, `select`, `warn`, `success`, `error`) and
`DLPressStyle` in `DLChat/Design/Components.swift`.

Status: **done** = changed in this pass; **there** = already in the app, checked and left;
**n/a** = does not apply, with the reason; **device** = built, but its feel needs a real iPhone.
Haptics cannot be felt in the Simulator: every haptic row also needs a device.
